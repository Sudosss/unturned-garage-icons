Unturned garage icons

Preview pictures of Unturned vehicles, published so that game servers can show players which vehicle a menu entry stands for. Pictures only: there are no mod files here. Every vehicle was made by its Steam Workshop author, who keeps all rights.

Each picture is a 256 x 256 transparent PNG at icons/<2 chars>/<guid>.png, where <guid> is the vehicle asset GUID as 32 lower-case hex characters and <2 chars> is its first two characters. The GUIDs that have a picture are listed one per line in manifest.txt.
